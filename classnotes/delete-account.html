Delete your account

Last updated: November 6, 2026

On this page

On this page

ClassNotes works without an account. If you made one, you can delete it at any time.

1. In the app

Open ClassNotes → Settings → your account → Delete account, and enter your password. It takes effect at once.

2. By email

If you can't open the app, email support@classmateapp.org from the address of the account and ask for it to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and password.
  • Your synced settings and your NOVA usage counts.
  • The copy of your library kept on NOVA Plus and Pro.
  • The notebooks you shared (their members lose access) and your memberships in other people's.

What you wrote in someone else's shared notebook stays part of their notebook. Backups of our database are deleted on their normal schedule.

4. What is not deleted

  • Your notebooks on your devices and in your own iCloud — they are your files. Delete them in the app (or delete the app) if you want them gone.
  • Your App Store subscription. Cancel NOVA Plus or Pro in the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, before or after deleting the account.
