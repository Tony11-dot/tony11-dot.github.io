Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 6, 2026

On this page

We want everyone to be able to use ClassNotes, including people with disabilities. This statement describes what ClassNotes and these pages do today, and how to tell us about a problem.

1. In the app

  • VoiceOver — buttons, tools and icons carry spoken labels, and the library, settings and NOVA can be used with VoiceOver.
  • Larger text — the app's text follows your Dynamic Type size.
  • Motion and transparency — with Reduce Motion on, animations such as the book opening become simple fades; with Reduce Transparency on, glass panels become solid.
  • Language — ClassNotes is in English. Handwriting search reads several languages on the device.

2. These pages

These pages are plain text that works with screen readers, keyboard navigation, browser zoom and dark mode.

3. Known limitations

Handwriting is a picture by nature: VoiceOver can read typed text and text boxes, but not handwritten ink unless it has been turned into text. Content that people add themselves — photos, files, voice notes — may not be accessible. If something is hard to use, tell us and we will help.

4. Contact

For accessibility problems, questions or requests, email support@classmateapp.org. Please describe the screen, the problem and the device you use. We aim to reply within 14 days.
