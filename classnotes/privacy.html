ClassNotes is a note-taking app for iPad and iPhone, made by Tony Aboud ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: without an account, ClassNotes sends nothing of yours to us. Your notebooks live on your device and, if you keep iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud. We never sell your data, show you ads or track you across apps.

1. Without an account

  • Your notebooks — ink, typed text, photos, files, voice notes, covers and settings — are stored on your device, as documents you own.
  • iCloud sync is on by default: copies of your notebooks are kept in your own iCloud Drive so your other devices can show them. Apple stores them under your Apple Account; we cannot see them. Turn it off in Settings → iCloud Sync.
  • Handwriting recognition and search run on your device, with Apple's own text recognition.
  • Code blocks you run in a notebook run on your device, offline. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit — stay on your device. They reach us only if you choose to send them (Settings → Help & Support → Support → Diagnostics), and they contain no note content.
  • No tracking. ClassNotes has no advertising, no analytics library and no tracking, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

2. If you create an account

An account is optional. It is needed for NOVA, for shared notebooks and for NOVA's plans.

  • Account details — your email address, your name and your password, which we store only as a one-way hash. To confirm the address is yours, we email you a 6-digit code (stored only as a hash, and valid for 30 minutes).
  • Your settings — your pens, colours, notebook types, and the layout of your library (your shelves' names, colours and icons, and which notebooks are on which shelf) — are stored with your account so they follow you to your other devices.
  • Usage counts — how many NOVA answers your account used each day, and on paid plans what they cost us. Counts only, never what was asked.
  • Security records — a count of recent wrong passwords for your account (to stop guessing), and password-reset links, stored only as hashes and valid for one hour.

3. NOVA, the study assistant — only with your permission

Nothing is sent to an AI model until you agree in the app; you can withdraw that in Settings → NOVA. When you ask NOVA something, ClassNotes sends:

  • your question and the conversation so far;
  • the part of the page you circled, as a picture, if you circled one;
  • text from a notebook's pages — only after you choose "Read this notebook", and only in that chat.

It goes through our server to the company whose model answers: Groq on the free plan, or Anthropic (Claude) on NOVA Plus and Pro, up to the plan's daily answers and on Groq after that. Your name, email address and account are not sent to them. We do not store your questions or NOVA's answers; on the free plan, what the model read off a circled picture is kept in our server's memory for up to 30 minutes, so follow-up questions about it don't send it again, and is then discarded. Your chats with NOVA are kept on your device. See Groq's privacy policy and Anthropic's privacy policy.

If NOVA's server can't answer or can't be reached, an iPad or iPhone with Apple Intelligence may answer the question itself with Apple's on-device model. That answer is labelled “Answered on this iPad”, and nothing it reads leaves your device.

4. Shared notebooks — only the ones you share

  • When you share a notebook, its content — ink, text and other page content, page settings, and photos, files and voice notes in it — is sent to our server and passed to the people you invited, who keep a copy on their devices. The server keeps the notebook's change history so members can catch up after being offline.
  • The invitation email carries your name and the notebook's title, nothing of what is in it.
  • People in a shared notebook see each other's names and email addresses. While it is open, who is in it, where they are on the page and what they are drawing is passed live to the others and never stored.
  • You can remove someone or stop sharing at any time; stopping sharing deletes the notebook's copy and history from our server. Copies already on members' devices are theirs to keep or delete.

5. NOVA Plus and Pro, answer packs and storage

  • Purchases — plans, answer packs and the storage add-on — are made through Apple and recorded by RevenueCat under your ClassNotes account's id, so they follow your account. We see what is active, how many pack answers you have used and when a subscription renews or ends — never your payment details.
  • A copy of your library is part of NOVA Plus, NOVA Pro and the storage add-on: pictures of your pages and covers, your notebooks' titles and shelves, and their voice notes, files and links are kept on our server so your other signed-in devices can show them, up to the room your purchase includes. It is removed 30 days after the last of them ends.
  • Pack answers are answered by Anthropic's Claude, exactly as a plan's Claude answers are (section 3).

6. Who receives data

Only the services needed to run these features:

  • Railway — hosts our server and its database, in the United States.
  • Cloudflare — carries the app's connection to our server.
  • Resend — sends our emails (codes, password resets, invitations).
  • Groq and Anthropic — answer NOVA questions (section 3).
  • RevenueCat and Apple — handle purchases (section 5).

Like any server, ours and these services see your device's IP address when the app connects. We use it only to stop abuse, such as too many attempts in a short time, and we do not keep it with your account. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account, settings and usage counts — until you delete the account.
  • The library copy on NOVA Plus and Pro — until 30 days after the plan ends, or until you delete the account.
  • A shared notebook — until its owner stops sharing it or deletes their account.
  • Codes and reset links stop working after 30 and 60 minutes.
  • Backups of our database are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

8. Deleting your data

Delete your account in ClassNotes (Settings → your account → Delete account), or email support@classmateapp.org from the account's address. Deleting removes the account and everything of it on our server: settings, usage counts, the library copy, the notebooks you shared (their members lose access) and your memberships in others'. What you wrote in someone else's shared notebook stays part of their notebook. Notebooks on your devices and in your iCloud are yours and are not touched. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel it in your Apple Account settings. More on deleting.

9. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used, by emailing support@classmateapp.org. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases: providing the service you asked for (your account, sync, sharing and plans), your consent (NOVA), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and free of abuse. You may complain to your local data protection authority, or in Israel to the Privacy Protection Authority.

10. Children

You can use ClassNotes without an account at any age, and then nothing of yours is sent to us. To create an account you must be at least 13, or older where your country's law requires a parent's consent below that age. ClassNotes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold data from them; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords, codes and reset links are stored only as hashes. Your sign-in on the device is kept in Apple's Keychain. Every request to our server is checked against your own account, so nobody can read another person's data. Nothing you write in a notebook is written to our logs.

12. International transfers

Our server is in the United States, and the services in section 6 may process data in other countries. We use only services that protect it as this policy describes.

13. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app.
